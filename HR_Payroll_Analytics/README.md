# 👥 Project 2: Automated HR Operations, Attendance & Payroll Tracker

## 📌 Business Overview
This project presents an automated HR Operations & Payroll management model designed to streamline payroll processing, calculate variable deductions based on attendance, and compute net salary disbursements across multiple departments.

---

## 🛠️ Excel Tools & Technical Features Demonstrated
* **Multi-Sheet Data Integration:** Linked `Employee_Master`, `Attendance_Summary`, and `Payroll_Dashboard` sheets.
* **Automated Deductions & Allowances:** Formulated Unpaid Leave Deductions based on daily salary rates and Overtime Pay calculations.
* **Statutory Compliance:** Standardized 12% Provident Fund (PF) auto-deduction logic.
* **Executive Presentation:** Gridless design formatted for HR leadership reporting.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & Metrics
* **Total Workforce Tracked:** 10 Employees
* **Key Functionalities:** Automated Gross-to-Net Salary computation, leave penalty tracking, and overtime compensation.
